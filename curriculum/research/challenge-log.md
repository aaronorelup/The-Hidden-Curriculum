# Challenge log, 2026-10-12

The challenge pass ran four lenses over `concepts.md`: **agent-does-it** (is the human being given a job the agent now does?), **invisible-failures** (what fails silently that no concept covers?), **prerequisites** (what does the inventory assume the learner already knows?) and **beyond-code** (what do people who ship media, apps, games, devices and automations miss?). It produced 40 findings. This file lists each one and what was done with it in `concepts.md` and `sources.md`.

## How the findings were applied

- **Every source was fetched and read on 2026-10-12** by the fix agent, not taken from the finding. Quotes were checked against the page; where a finding's quote or claim was not exactly what the page says, the concept uses the page's wording and the correction is noted below.
- **New sources are S206-S251** in `sources.md`, each with its date (or "undated" and why). Existing sources re-cited for new points were re-opened (S029, S042, S050, S052, S056, S057, S063, S069, S071, S074, S117, S142, S146, S149, S154, S174, S195; the S002 and S003 transcripts were downloaded and the cited lines checked).
- **New concepts are C117-C135.** G13 "The ground floor" is a new group (C117, C122-C130), taught first though its IDs come last. The others joined existing groups: C118 (G01), C120 (G02), C134 (G06), C119, C121 and C131 (G07), C135 (G10), C132 and C133 (G12).
- **Nothing was deleted silently.** No whole concept was retired. Every replaced point is quoted, with the reason, in the new "Retired concepts" section at the end of `concepts.md`.
- **Mechanical changes:** the Contents list was regenerated from the headings; every bold "Bring in an expert" label (90, counting those in the new concepts) now points to C118; 31 existing concepts gained a **Requires** field naming the G13 concepts they assume; `sources.md` "Cited by" lists were recomputed from the text (union with the old lists), and its date statistics were updated (251 sources, 197 dated 2026, 145 from 2026-07 or later, 15 older, 39 undated).
- **Limits of this pass.** The shared web-search budget was used up before this agent started, so no new searches ran; every page was fetched directly by URL. OpenAI's data-use page and California's SB 243 page both returned HTTP 403. Reddit blocks the fetcher.
