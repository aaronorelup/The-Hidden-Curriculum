# 10.16 media: every exhibit

Prepared 2026-10-04 by the media-prep agent (Opus 5.5) from `_planning/opus-made-this/` (experiments, QA.md, ASSET_INDEX.md, every card.json, assets/existing captions). Machine-readable list: `media.json` (one entry per exhibit, with the full caption, licence, source path and card). Every frame of every Jefrie clip was looked at (tiled), and every other clip at sampled frames.

**Total: 48.30 MB** for the 75 exhibits below and 14 data files (video 25 files, 41.95 MB; images 34, 1.99 MB; audio 16 clips with their spectrograms, 4.25 MB). The whole `assets/` folder is 55.61 MB, including 7.31 MB of X-post clips that the x-clips agent prepared (listed in `assets/x_clips.json`, not here). Largest single file: 6.08 MB.

**Encoding.** Video: H.264 High, yuv420p (limited range), GOP 12 (`-g 12 -keyint_min 12 -sc_threshold 0`), no B-frames, +faststart; grid clips at 960x540 (Moonlight 960x402, its 2.39:1 frame), full-screen clips at 1280x720; MiniMax-H3 clips stay at their native 736x416 (not upscaled); source frame rates kept (24 or 30), 60 fps game footage halved to 30. Muted unless `sound` is true (the two games keep their own audio, linear-gained to -16 LUFS). Stills: WebP at display size. Audio: 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 192 kbps; integrated loudness measured, then one linear gain to -16.0 LUFS (re-trimmed on the encoded MP3 to within 0.05 LU); every clip lands at -15.98 to -16.02 LUFS. Spectrograms: `showspectrumpic=s=1600x400:legend=0:mode=combined:color=intensity:scale=log:fscale=lin:win_func=hann:stop=11025` (0-11 kHz), the same for all.

**Captions.** Claude dollar figures are API-list equivalents (Aaron pays a subscription). Each caption names the exact model; the early toy Jefries were Opus 5 and Fable 5.1, not Opus 5.5.
