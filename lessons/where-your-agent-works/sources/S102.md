# S102 — Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude

**Credit:** Anthropic, "Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude", Claude blog (Announcements; Claude apps, Claude Cowork), 2026-09-16. https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude (also served at https://claude.com/resources/articles/cowork-is-now-claude). No named author; corporate post. Re-read in full 2026-10-14 (page live, unchanged date).

Kind: vendor announcement (product news, not a study). Nothing in it is measured; every claim is Anthropic describing its own product.

## What the lesson uses, checked against the post

1. **The merge, dated.** Announced 2026-09-16: "Starting today, Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude." Spec's quoted fragment ("Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude") is verbatim.
2. **Rollout, not a switch.** "rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks, with more plans to follow" (verbatim in the post). Later paragraph: Pro and Max first, "in the Claude app on web, desktop, and mobile", to existing and new users on those plans; nothing to turn on. Team and Free "will follow soon"; Enterprise admins get at least 30 days' notice before anything changes for their organisations. So as of the post, Team, Free and Enterprise do not have it.
3. **Why they merged.** Anthropic says it built Cowork "as a separate place for bigger work" and Design for visual work; users said the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged, and work started in one did not carry to the other. "So we stopped making you choose."
4. **Claude decides what a request needs.** The post's words: "Claude can now figure out what a task needs", so Cowork and Design capabilities are "available from any conversation, with the context, skills, and connectors you already have". Subtitle: "You don't have to choose where a task goes." The quick-question vs. handed-over-task contrast is the post's own example ("Bring a quick question, or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there").
5. **Permissions default.** "By default, Claude asks before taking an action." (verbatim). The user can instead turn on a mode where Claude keeps working and checks in only "when something needs a closer look"; "You keep the final say."
6. **Keeps going after you leave.** "...even after you've closed your laptop" (first paragraph); you can check progress from your phone; tasks can be scheduled (example: a weekly report every Monday, Claude starts without being asked). Useful one-line support for "the agent stops when I close my laptop" being wrong, alongside S129/S184.
7. **What carries over.** Cowork users keep chats, projects, artifacts, connectors and skills. Chat-only users "don't have to do anything different".
8. **Same day, also new:** Claude Docs and Claude Slides; Claude Design inside conversations. All three in beta on paid plans; Enterprise admins choose when to enable; standalone Claude Design keeps working. (Out of this lesson's scope except as colour; media/tools belong elsewhere.)

## What the spec attributes to S102 that the post does NOT say

- **"Claude works out whether a request is a quick answer or a task"** (spec C022.3, You vs the agent): a fair paraphrase, but not the post's wording and not a described mechanism. The post says Claude "can now figure out what a task needs" and gives the quick-question vs report example. Narrate it as a paraphrase, not as a quote; do not claim the post explains how the decision is made.
- **Where the work runs.** The post never says cloud, servers, local, VM or files leaving the machine. "Even after you've closed your laptop" implies off-device execution but does not state it. The cloud-by-default fact (2026-10-06) must be credited to S184/S069, not S102.
- **Desktop tabs.** The post says nothing about Chat/Cowork/Code tabs disappearing, nor anything about Claude Code. "Show whichever the viewer's app has" rests on S208 plus the rollout wording here.
- **Framing "Chat and Cowork are separate modes you pick" is outdated (S102):** supported only for Pro/Max accounts that have received the rollout; for Team, Free and Enterprise it is still how the app works until their rollout. Say so.
